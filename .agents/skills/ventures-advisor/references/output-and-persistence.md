# Ventures Outputs and Persistence

## Response shape

Adapt to the request. A substantial initiative answer can use:

```markdown
## Current context

## Evidence

## Important unknowns

## Constraints

## Alternatives and trade-offs

## Recommendation

## Next useful step

## What would change the recommendation
```

Do not force every heading onto a simple lookup. Keep retrieved evidence,
interpretation, unknowns, and recommendation visibly distinct. Identify stale,
reviewable, disputed, historical, and unconfirmed evidence where it matters.

## Comparisons and recommendations

For a comparison, state the objective and explicit constraints first. For each
alternative, identify supported advantages, conflicts, commitments, and missing
evidence. A recommendation should be conditional and explainable:

- it is derived from the retrieved evidence;
- it does not prove demand, viability, success, authority, ownership, or intent;
- it does not invent deadlines, customers, revenue, or adoption;
- it does not change a project lifecycle, goal, preference, or commitment; and
- it says what new evidence or user decision would change it.

A suggestion for a next step is not an executed commitment. The user remains
the person who adopts a plan, makes a decision, or creates a commitment.

## Cross-vertical output

Keep the owning page attached to each claim:

- Ventures: initiative lifecycle, state, decisions, milestones, outcomes, and
  project-specific commitments;
- Career: professional relevance and evidence of scope or impact;
- Learning: knowledge state and demonstrated understanding;
- Relationships: interpersonal history and relationship commitments;
- Writing: communication fit and generated project messaging; and
- Core: broad constraints and preferences.

Use links or path references instead of copying the complete fact into another
vertical. A task packet may combine relevant context but remains derived,
scoped, and ephemeral unless explicitly retained.

## Persistence decision

Ordinary lookups, comparisons, prioritization answers, opportunity assessments,
and next-step suggestions remain ephemeral. Evaluate a durable result only when:

- the user explicitly asks to remember, retain, or reuse it;
- it captures a non-obvious comparison likely to be reused;
- it exposes a meaningful unresolved project question; or
- SelfContext's normal ingest or query procedure identifies durable continuity
  value.

Before persisting, SelfContext must:

1. search Ventures and `derived/` for an existing matching record or synthesis;
2. classify facts, source material, observations, recommendations, and unknowns;
3. check ownership and link Career, Learning, Relationships, Writing, Core, and
   Sources rather than copying their claims;
4. compare the result with current goals, constraints, review items, and stale
   evidence; and
5. record a freshness limitation when current state materially affects reuse.

If justified, store only a small `type: synthesis` page with
`assertion_kind: derived_synthesis`, provenance links, and a body that labels
recommendations as recommendations. Do not store an Advisor response as a
venture/project fact, commitment, goal, preference, or decision. Do not create a
page merely because the answer was long or useful once.

## Privacy and refusal boundary

Do not retain credentials, tokens, secrets, API keys, authentication material,
complete private workspace exports, wholesale messages, or unnecessary
third-party personal information. Refuse requests to infer collaborator motives,
reliability, sensitive characteristics, employment, compensation, equity,
ownership, authority, or contractual status from weak evidence. Offer a
safe, minimum-context alternative such as the evidenced initiative role,
reported proposal, or explicit unknown.

If a proposal, discussion, or interest is not an agreement, say so. If status is
stale, say it needs confirmation rather than calling it false. If the evidence
is missing, say what cannot be established instead of completing the narrative.
